Club Link

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Club Link is a small student club website. This policy explains what information the site uses and why. It is written for club members, officers, and visitors.

Information shown publicly

Schedule details, events, announcements, club descriptions, membership instructions, and any club contact address posted by an officer are public. Officers should not publish private student information in these areas.

Officer account information

Officer accounts use Supabase Authentication. Supabase stores the email address, password credential, and session information needed to sign in. Club Link does not display officer email addresses to the public and does not store passwords in its own database tables.

Private meeting notes are stored separately from the public schedule. Database access policies restrict those notes to authorized officers. Officers should still avoid putting sensitive student information in meeting notes unless the club has a clear reason and permission to keep it there.

Technical information

The website host, Supabase, Google Fonts, and the jsDelivr content delivery service may process basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, request time, and error logs, to deliver site files and maintain the service. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

How information is used

  • To display current club information to members.
  • To authenticate officers and check whether an account is authorized.
  • To prevent unauthorized changes and troubleshoot site problems.

Club Link does not include advertising, sell personal information, or create public member profiles.

Data choices and questions

Members can use the public website without creating an account. An officer who needs an account corrected or removed should contact the club administrator using the contact information listed on the dashboard. Questions about a public post should also be directed to the club.

Changes to this policy

If the club changes how Club Link handles information, this page should be updated with a new effective date and a clear description of the change.
