Club Link

Terms of Use

Effective September 7, 2026

Club Link provides meeting, event, announcement, and membership information for a student club. By using the site, you agree to use it responsibly and for its intended club-related purpose.

Public information

Club schedules can change. Members should check the latest post and confirm time-sensitive details with a club officer when necessary. Club Link is an information tool and does not replace official school notices.

Officer accounts

Officer access is limited to people authorized by the club administrator. Account holders must keep their credentials private, use accurate club information, and sign out on shared devices. A user may not attempt to access another account or bypass the site's authorization controls.

Acceptable use

Do not use Club Link to disrupt the site, test it without permission, upload harmful content, impersonate another person, or publish private, unlawful, or unrelated material. Public posts should be appropriate for the school community.

Content updates and removal

Authorized officers may edit or remove club content to keep information current. Access to an officer account may be removed when a person no longer holds an authorized role or if the account is misused.

Availability

The club may update, pause, or stop the site when needed. Although reasonable care should be taken to keep information and access available, uninterrupted service is not promised.

Questions

Questions about these terms or information posted on Club Link should be sent to the club contact listed on the dashboard.
